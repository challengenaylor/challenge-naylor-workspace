What this tells you, and how to use it

The 4 cards below answer one question each: "If nothing unusual happens, roughly where does my balance land in 7 / 30 / 90 / 180 days?" They're built from your actual weekday spending/income patterns plus every fuel invoice, other payment, deposit, and recurring bill you've entered — not a guess.

Use it for: deciding whether to say yes to a delivery, whether to request an extension, and spotting a squeeze coming weeks out — the kind of question a single bank statement can't answer.

Don't use it for: an exact balance on an exact future date. It's a rule-based average of your history, not a crystal ball — see the accuracy note on each card, and check Reports → Forecast Accuracy for the real, back-tested error rate on your own data. The further out the horizon, the wider that error naturally gets.

Income Patterns learned separately by source — EFTPOS, Z Card settlements, cash deposits, customer payments — not one blended average
